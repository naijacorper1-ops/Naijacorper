Add Opportunities, Events and PPA Reviews modules

- Opportunities: admins, recruiters and businesses publish directly;
  verified corps members submit for approval. Filters, my posts,
  open/close, edit with re-review, owner delete.
- Events: admins publish directly; recruiters, businesses and verified
  corps members submit for approval. RSVP with counts, attendee list for
  creator and admins, cancel with attendee notifications, cover upload.
- PPA reviews: verified corps members only, one review per PPA,
  anonymous by default, ratings recomputed from review rows,
  find-or-create PPAs by normalised name and state.
- Admin: queues, approve/reject, remove/restore, PPA review removal,
  new report targets, new stats keys, audit actions.
- Additive migration only. Account deletion now also fixes PPA totals,
  RSVP counts and event cover images.
- Unit and end-to-end tests, regenerated API docs, patch notes 3.
